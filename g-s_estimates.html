Everything on Estimates

26 steps · 3:31 video Every button

In the office app, press Help and type everything on estimates: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 5, Estimates, at 22:36
  1. 1

    Estimates

    Every estimate in one list, from the first visit to the won job: drafts, ones out with customers, follow-ups, won and lost.

  2. 2

    Start a new estimate

    New estimate first asks who it is for. The tour opens it to show you.

  3. 3

    Find the customer

    Type a name, phone or street and click the customer: a new draft estimate opens for them straight away.

  4. 4

    Not a customer yet?

    Add a new customer opens the customer form right here. Saving it adds the customer and opens their new estimate.

  5. 5

    Name

    First and last name, or a company name for commercial work. Only a name is required.

  6. 6

    Phones and email

    Cell phone, other phone and email. The email is where the estimate and its follow-ups go.

  7. 7

    Property address

    Where the work is. Type the zip and the town fills itself in. Untick "Bill to the property address" if the bill goes somewhere else.

  8. 8

    Source, salesperson and CSR

    How they found you, who referred them, their salesperson and the office CSR. Stage, type, invoicing and payment terms sit just below.

  9. 9

    Book the visit in the same go

    Tick it to put the estimate visit on the salesperson's day with the same save: date, time, who goes and what for.

  10. 10

    Save customer

    Saves the customer and opens their new estimate. The tour doesn't save anything.

  11. 11

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving.

  12. 12

    Drafts

    Estimates written but not sent yet, and what they are worth. Click it to see them on the Open tab.

  13. 13

    Out with customers

    Sent and waiting on a yes or no, with their total value.

  14. 14

    In follow-up

    Sent estimates with a follow-up due or planned. With automatic follow-ups on, the reminder emails go by themselves.

  15. 15

    Won this year

    What you won this year, how many jobs that is, and your close rate (won out of won plus lost).

  16. 16

    Open

    The list starts on Open: drafts, and sent estimates still waiting on an answer. The number is how many.

  17. 17

    Follow-ups

    Sent estimates with a follow-up due, or the next automatic email planned.

  18. 18

    Who to chase

    The Status column says Follow up, 2nd follow-up or Call them, and shows the next automatic step with its date.

  19. 19

    Won

    Every estimate the customer said yes to.

  20. 20

    Straight to the job

    Job opens the job that was made from that estimate.

  21. 21

    Lost

    Lost and expired estimates, with the reason given when they were marked lost.

  22. 22

    All

    Every estimate, whatever its status.

  23. 23

    Search

    Type an estimate number, a title, the customer or the town: the list narrows as you type.

  24. 24

    Filter by rep

    Show one salesperson's estimates only. Any rep shows everyone's.

  25. 25

    The columns

    Estimate number, title and customer; status; rep; total; and when it was sent. A long list shows 50 at a time with Previous and Next.

  26. 26

    Open one

    Click any row to open the estimate. The tour "Everything on an estimate" walks through it.
