Set up a payment schedule (deposit, progress payments, final)

19 steps · 2:16 video Owner, Office

In the office app, press Help and type set up a payment schedule: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Payment schedules

    Each estimate picks one, by type of work. A fixed first deposit is allowed; the percentages share what is left.

  2. 2

    New schedule

    Opens the editor with a 30 / 60 / 10 start.

  3. 3

    Name

    What it's called on the estimate, e.g. Paver / hardscape (7 payments).

  4. 4

    Default for these types of work

    Crew specializations, with commas (hardscape, landscape…). Estimates for that work start with this schedule.

  5. 5

    The default schedule

    Tick it for the schedule every other estimate starts with.

  6. 6

    Start from

    Ready-made schedules: 30 / 60 / 10, 50 / 50, Deposit + final, Due on receipt. Pick one, then change it.

  7. 7

    Deposit + final

    For example a fixed deposit, then the balance.

  8. 8

    One payment

    Its number and name, as the customer reads it.

  9. 9

    How much

    % of the rest (a share of what's left after fixed amounts), Fixed $ or The balance (whatever is left; only one).

  10. 10

    The amount

    Dollars for a fixed payment, a percentage for "% of the rest".

  11. 11

    When it's due

    At signing, on receipt, days before the start, when work starts, on completion, or on a date.

  12. 12

    Order

    The arrows move a payment up or down; the x removes it.

  13. 13

    Add a payment

    Adds another payment at the end.

  14. 14

    Does it add up?

    Green when it works. Red says what to fix, e.g. percentages that don't make 100%.

  15. 15

    Save schedule

    Saves it. Open estimates can pick it straight away.

  16. 16

    Close the editor

    The tour closes it without saving.

  17. 17

    Change one

    Click a schedule to change it.

  18. 18

    Delete

    Removes it after asking. Open estimates using it go back to their default; signed contracts keep their payments.

  19. 19

    Close it

    The tour closes it without saving.
