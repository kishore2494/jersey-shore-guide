Everything on an estimate

25 steps · 4:23 video Every button

In the office app, press Help and type everything on an estimate: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 5, Estimates, at 26:01
  1. 1

    Open an estimate

    Click a row to open the estimate. The tour opens this one.

  2. 2

    Back to the customer

    The customer's name above the title opens their page: phones, notes, and all their estimates, jobs and invoices.

  3. 3

    Where it stands

    The number and status sit above the title. Here: the status (Draft, Sent, Won, Lost), any follow-up due, the address, phone and when it was sent.

  4. 4

    Customer view

    Switch to see the estimate exactly as the customer gets it.

  5. 5

    The real document

    The saved copy the customer receives by email, as a PDF or on paper: sections, totals, add-ons and terms. Print / PDF is just above it.

  6. 6

    Back to Build

    Build is where you write the estimate.

  7. 7

    Save

    Saves sections, prices and details. "Unsaved changes" shows beside it until you do. ⌘S or Ctrl+S saves too.

  8. 8

    Send

    Opens the send choices: email the online link (best), email it, print or save as PDF, or "I sent it another way". Each marks it Sent and starts the follow-ups.

  9. 9

    Won

    When the customer says yes. One step makes the contract, the payment schedule and the job. The tour "Mark an estimate won or lost" shows it.

  10. 10

    Lost

    When they say no: pick why (price, someone else, timing…). It stays for the close-rate report, and Reopen brings it back as a draft.

  11. 11

    Measure

    Opens a satellite photo of the property. Trace the patio, driveway, beds or wall line; back here a Measured card turns each area into a section.

  12. 12

    Duplicate

    Copies this estimate as a new draft for the same customer, e.g. a second option, and opens the copy.

  13. 13

    Sections

    Each section is one line the customer sees (Patio, Walls, Lighting…) with its own total. The labor price and the materials go inside it.

  14. 14

    Taxable or Tax exempt

    One click switches the section between Taxable and Tax exempt; the sales tax in Totals follows.

  15. 15

    Materials, hidden or shown

    The materials in a section. Unticked lines are hidden: the customer sees only the section total. Cost and price give you the margin.

  16. 16

    Optional add-ons

    A dashed section is an optional add-on: priced on its own and left out of the total until the customer takes it.

  17. 17

    Add a section

    Search the catalogue: a service brings its price, unit, crew-hours, tax setting and its kit of materials. Blank section starts an empty one.

  18. 18

    Totals

    Subtotal, sales tax at this estimate's rate and the total. Optional add-ons are shown apart, not in the total.

  19. 19

    Customer copy

    Open the customer copy shows the document full width; Print / PDF prints it or saves a PDF you can attach to your own email.

  20. 20

    Contract terms

    The payment schedule the contract gets, what each payment comes to, and the NJ ST-8 capital improvement switch (no sales tax).

  21. 21

    Online estimate

    The customer's link: Sent, Opened, Signed. They tick add-ons, initial the terms and sign; signing makes the job, contract and deposit invoice.

  22. 22

    Follow-ups

    What the automatic follow-up does next and when, with an on / off switch for this estimate. Emails and texts about it are listed here.

  23. 23

    Margin

    Materials at cost plus crew-hours at your labor cost: cost, gross profit, margin % and crew-hours. Never shown to the customer.

  24. 24

    Material selections

    What the customer picked at the supplier: pavers, wall block, caps, steps… It goes on the selection sheet, the proposal and the work order.

  25. 25

    Details

    Title, salesperson, valid until, tax %, a message to the customer, an office note they never see, and terms. Save keeps your changes.
