Take a cleanup sign-up on the phone

11 steps Owner, Office, Sales

In the office app, press Help and type take a cleanup sign-up on the phone: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Cleanup sign-ups

    Phone sign-ups are taken here.

  2. 2

    Take a phone sign-up

    On the season's card. The tour opens the Spring one.

  3. 3

    Who is calling

    Name, phone and email. Name and phone are required.

  4. 4

    Where and when

    The service address and town, and their preferred date or time frame (a range, now, a month, no preference).

  5. 5

    What they want

    Tick the services from your form; Other is for anything not on the list.

  6. 6

    Estimate style

    Meet a manager (an estimate visit is booked) or just send a quote (a stop-by to quote).

  7. 7

    Auto-pay call

    Spring form: would they like a call about auto-pay? Yes turns into a separate call for the office.

  8. 8

    Billing changes and notes

    A separate billing address or new contact details, and anything else they mention.

  9. 9

    Add to the list

    Adds them in line with their number, like the online form. The tour doesn't save.

  10. 10

    Close the form

    The x (or Cancel) closes it without saving.

  11. 11

    Work the list from #1

    Accept books the estimate visit or the stop-by quote; the x dismisses. Requests are handled in the order received.
