Send an estimate and follow it up

16 steps · 2:17 video Money

In the office app, press Help and type send an estimate and follow it up: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 5, Estimates, at 41:10
  1. 1

    Open the estimate

    Sending starts on the estimate. The tour opens this one.

  2. 2

    Send (or Send again)

    Opens: Email the online link (best: they sign on their phone), Email it, Print / save as PDF, or I sent it another way. Each marks it Sent.

  3. 3

    Check it first

    The Customer copy card shows exactly what they will get.

  4. 4

    Open the customer copy

    The tour opens it to show you.

  5. 5

    The customer's copy

    Your letterhead, each section with its total, shown materials, optional add-ons and the terms: exactly what they receive.

  6. 6

    Print / PDF

    Opens the print window. Choose Save as PDF to attach it to your own email.

  7. 7

    Close it

    The x closes the copy.

  8. 8

    The online estimate

    The best way to send: the customer opens a link, ticks the add-ons they want, initials the terms and signs.

  9. 9

    Sent, Opened, Signed

    Where the customer is. Signing makes the job, the contract and the deposit invoice by itself.

  10. 10

    The link

    How many add-ons it offers, and the link itself.

  11. 11

    Copy the link

    Copies it, to paste into a text message or your own email.

  12. 12

    See it as they do

    Opens the link as the customer sees it.

  13. 13

    Email the link again

    Re-sends the same link. If you change the estimate after it went out, a yellow note offers Send the update, which replaces their link.

  14. 14

    Follow-ups

    After sending, reminders go by themselves: email 1, email 2, then a call ticket for the office. Days are set in Automations.

  15. 15

    Automatic on or off

    Switch automatic follow-ups off for this estimate only; it takes effect at once. The company-wide switch is on Automations.

  16. 16

    What happens next

    The next automatic step and when it goes. Messages already sent are listed under it. Follow-ups due also show on My day.
