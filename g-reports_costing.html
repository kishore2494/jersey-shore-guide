Guide / Money

Job costing: profit by job, salesperson and crew

22 steps Money

In the office app, press Help and type job costing: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Job costing

    Open the Job costing tab on Reports.

  2. 2

    Pick the dates

    This month, last month, this year, last year or the last 12 months. The dates in use show next to it.

  3. 3

    Last 12 months

    Click a range and the whole tab recalculates for it.

  4. 4

    Revenue

    Contracts plus approved change orders for jobs with costs. Jobs with no crew time or costs yet aren't counted, so they don't show a false 100%.

  5. 5

    Job costs

    Crew hours at the burn rate, plus materials and subcontractors.

  6. 6

    Gross profit

    Revenue minus job costs, and the margin.

  7. 7

    Crew hours

    Hours the crews logged against the hours estimated. Going over is what eats the margin.

  8. 8

    Job profit

    Jobs finished in the range plus those still running.

  9. 9

    One job

    CO = change orders in the revenue. Red hours = more than estimated. Margin shows the estimate's margin next to it. Click a job to open it.

  10. 10

    By salesperson

    The same numbers added up per salesperson.

  11. 11

    By crew

    Per crew. A job two crews worked on counts for both.

  12. 12

    Back to jobs

    One row per job again.

  13. 13

    Export

    Downloads every job as an Excel file: estimated and actual hours, labor, material, subs, other, total cost, profit and margins.

  14. 14

    Burn rates

    What one man-hour costs: wages, payroll taxes, workers' comp, benefits and overhead. Types of work without their own rate use the company-wide one.

  15. 15

    Rate cards

    Each card: the rate today and since when. A rate that starts later shows in amber; History lists older rates.

  16. 16

    New rate

    Owner and office can add a rate, for example when wages or comp change.

  17. 17

    For

    Company-wide, or one type of work such as Hardscape or Landscape.

  18. 18

    Per man-hour

    The full cost of one man-hour, all in.

  19. 19

    From

    Work from this date costs at the new rate. Earlier work keeps the rate it had.

  20. 20

    Note

    What's in it, for example '2027 wages + comp'.

  21. 21

    Save rate

    Saves the rate and job profit recalculates. The tour doesn't save it.

  22. 22

    Close

    The X closes the form without saving.
