A quick tour of the app

8 steps · 1:09 video

In the office app, press Help and type a quick tour of the app: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    My day

    Every morning starts here: your appointments, follow-ups due, new requests, jobs waiting on a permit and who is on site.

  2. 2

    Search everything

    Type a name, phone, street or an EST-, JOB- or INV- number. ⌘K jumps here from any screen.

  3. 3

    Ask for help any time

    Tell it what you want to do and it shows you, step by step, like this.

  4. 4

    Customers

    Every customer and lead. One screen each: phones, addresses, notes, estimates, jobs, invoices.

  5. 5

    Estimates

    Every estimate and its follow-ups. Won ones become jobs in one step.

  6. 6

    Schedule

    Crews down the side, days across. Drag work onto a crew; drag it again to move it.

  7. 7

    Invoices

    Bills, payments, card declines and month-end billing.

  8. 8

    The Guide

    Every how-to in one place. Each one has a tour like this.
