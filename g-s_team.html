Guide / Setup

Everything on Team & crews

23 steps Every buttonOwner, Office
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on team & crews: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Team & crews

    Who works here and the crews the schedule plans with. Only the owner and the office open it. The line under the title counts crews and active people.

  2. 2

    New crew

    Adds a crew: name, kind of work, lead, size, hours a day, colour and members. The tour "Set up a crew" shows each part.

  3. 3

    Add person

    Gives someone a role in the office app or the crew phone app.

  4. 4

    Name

    Their name as it shows on appointments, crews and the schedule.

  5. 5

    Login id

    Their FieldProxy login (lower case, no spaces). It ties the person to their login and can't be changed later.

  6. 6

    Role

    Owner, Office, Sales, Project manager, Crew lead or Field: it decides what they can open. For a new person, the four office roles tick Takes appointments.

  7. 7

    Phone

    Their phone number.

  8. 8

    Hourly cost

    What an hour of their time costs you; job costing uses it. Only people who see money see this.

  9. 9

    Sees prices and money

    Ticked: they see prices, margins, invoices and payments. Unticked: all of that is hidden from them.

  10. 10

    Takes appointments

    Ticked: they're in the "With" list when you book a visit or a phone call. A task can go to anyone.

  11. 11

    Office app or phone app

    Crew leads and field staff use the crew phone app and never see money there. Office roles open this app.

  12. 12

    Add person

    Saves them. Cancel, or the X at the top, closes without saving.

  13. 13

    Close the form

    The tour closes it without saving.

  14. 14

    Crews

    Your crews, one card each.

  15. 15

    A crew

    Its colour on the schedule, kind of work, lead, people × hours a day = the crew-hours a day the schedule checks against, and members (★ = lead). Click it to change it.

  16. 16

    People

    Everyone with a role: login ("no login yet" until they have one), role, sees money, takes appointments, the crew they lead and phone. Inactive people are tagged.

  17. 17

    Hourly cost

    What each person's hour costs, for job costing. Only people who see money see this column.

  18. 18

    Change a person

    Click a person to change their role, access or phone.

  19. 19

    Login id stays

    Once saved, the login id can't be changed.

  20. 20

    Active

    Untick Active when someone leaves: they come off the pick lists and their history stays.

  21. 21

    Save

    Save keeps the changes.

  22. 22

    Close it

    The tour closes it without saving.

  23. 23

    Logins without a role

    FieldProxy logins that have no role here yet. Add each one with Add person, using the same login id, to let them in.
