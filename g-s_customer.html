Everything on a customer

27 steps Every button
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on a customer: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Open a customer

    Click a customer on the list to open their screen. Let's open Bayview Marina.

  2. 2

    Who they are

    Code (and Commercial), name and stage, then the property address, phone and email at a glance.

  3. 3

    Back to the list

    Goes back to the Customers list.

  4. 4

    Appointment

    Books a visit, call or task for them: their name, phone and address come along, and With starts as their salesperson.

  5. 5

    Estimate

    Starts a new draft estimate for this customer and opens it. The tour doesn't press it, because it makes the draft.

  6. 6

    Log call

    Writes down a phone call, a note or an email on their Timeline.

  7. 7

    Edit

    Opens their details in the same form as New customer. The tour "Book, log a call or edit a customer" opens these forms.

  8. 8

    Key facts

    Rep, CSR, where they came from, customer since, monthly invoicing and card on file. Do not market and Tax exempt show here in colour when set.

  9. 9

    Timeline

    Everything that happened with them, newest first: calls, appointments, estimates, emails, invoices and payments.

  10. 10

    Estimates

    Their estimates: number, status, any follow-up due, rep, total and date. Click one to open it.

  11. 11

    Jobs

    Their jobs: status, when it runs and the value. Click one to open the job.

  12. 12

    Invoices

    Their invoices: date issued, status, total and what's still open. Click one to open it.

  13. 13

    Appointments

    Their visits, calls and tasks. The number on the tab counts the open ones.

  14. 14

    New appointment

    Books one for this customer, the same as the Appointment button at the top.

  15. 15

    Open one

    Click a row to see it: when, who, notes and what came back from the visit (notes, photos), with Edit and Mark done.

  16. 16

    Edit it here

    The pencil changes it right here, without going to the Appointments screen.

  17. 17

    Mark done

    The tick closes it. A done one shows a Reopen arrow instead, to open it again.

  18. 18

    Photos

    Property photos and files kept on the customer, not buried in a job: permits, surveys, drawings.

  19. 19

    Add photos and files

    Take photo or Upload file adds one. Each file has Open, and the bin takes it off the customer (the file itself is kept).

  20. 20

    Messages

    Every email and text sent to them: follow-ups, reminders, notices, campaigns. Each shows Sent, Queued (with when it goes) or Failed.

  21. 21

    Account balance

    What they owe across all open invoices (or their credit), each open invoice (click one to open it) and what they've paid to date.

  22. 22

    Containers

    Dumpsters at this customer now: unit, size, address and days out. Drop one books a drop and Pickup books a pickup, both on Deliveries.

  23. 23

    Maintenance plans

    Recurring work like lawn care, beds or snow: how often, price and next visit. Add plan (money roles) opens Maintenance for this customer.

  24. 24

    What they get from us

    Seasonal marketing emails, payment reminders and text messages. A switch saves the moment you flip it, so change one only when the customer asks.

  25. 25

    Surveys

    Their latest survey scores out of 5, with comments. 3 or less shows in red.

  26. 26

    Coming up

    Their next open appointments. The tick marks one done.

  27. 27

    Contact

    Cell, other phone, email, property, bill-to address and terms. Office notes and other contacts show below when there are any.
