Write and send an estimate

8 steps · 1:11 video

In the office app, press Help and type write and send an estimate: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    A new estimate

    Start here, or from the customer with New estimate.

  2. 2

    Open one

    Let's look inside an estimate.

  3. 3

    One section per scope

    Paver patio, Lighting, Walls … The customer sees each section with its total; materials inside stay hidden unless you tick them.

  4. 4

    Taxable or tax exempt

    One click switches the section between Taxable and Tax exempt; the tax follows.

  5. 5

    Measure on the aerial photo

    Trace the patio, driveway, beds or wall line; the areas go onto the estimate in one click.

  6. 6

    Pavers and colours

    The customer's picks, and the Selection sheet for them to sign.

  7. 7

    What the customer gets

    Open or print exactly what the customer receives.

  8. 8

    Send it

    Top right: Email it (or Email the link again / Send the update once it has gone out) and Print / PDF. Follow-ups due then show on My day.
