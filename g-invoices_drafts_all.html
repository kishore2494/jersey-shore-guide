Guide / Money

Email or print all the draft invoices at once

3 steps Money

In the office app, press Help and type email or print all the draft invoices at once: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Drafts

    The invoices not sent yet, for example after the month-end run.

  2. 2

    Email all

    Emails each customer their own invoice, one after another. Any customer without an email address is listed so you can print theirs.

  3. 3

    Print all

    One print job with every draft, a page each; they're marked sent.
