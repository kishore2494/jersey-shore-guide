Guide / Money

Everything on Reports

26 steps · 3:46 video Every buttonMoney

In the office app, press Help and type everything on reports: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 14, Reports, at 1:58:24
  1. 1

    Reports

    The year's numbers: sales, salespeople, money owed, commissions and job profit. Only roles that see money can open it.

  2. 2

    Sales to date

    Invoiced this year before tax, and how it compares with the same point last year.

  3. 3

    Won this year

    Estimates won this year, before tax, and how many jobs.

  4. 4

    Close rate

    Won out of won plus lost estimates this year.

  5. 5

    Owed to us

    Everything open on invoices and how much is past due. Click it to jump to Money owed.

  6. 6

    Sales by month

    The Sales tab: this year's bars next to last year's, month by month, invoiced before tax. Hover a bar for the amount.

  7. 7

    Export

    Every table here has Export: it downloads the numbers as an Excel file.

  8. 8

    Won by category

    What sells: estimate sections won this year, by catalogue category (or the section name).

  9. 9

    Salespeople

    Each salesperson's year.

  10. 10

    Each salesperson

    Won and lost by decision date, jobs, close rate, and what's out now (open estimates: amount and how many). Export it for Excel.

  11. 11

    Money owed

    Who owes what, and how late.

  12. 12

    By age

    All customers together: Current, then 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and Over 90 days past due.

  13. 13

    Print the call list

    Every week or two: each customer who owes, oldest past due first, with phone numbers and room to note the call. The tour doesn't print.

  14. 14

    By customer

    One row per customer, split by age, with the total. Click a customer to open them. Export it for Excel.

  15. 15

    Commissions

    What salespeople have earned.

  16. 16

    Earned and paid

    Per salesperson: earned but not paid yet, and paid this year. The line above says how it's worked out; rates are in Settings, Money.

  17. 17

    Mark paid

    After you pay a salesperson, Mark paid moves all their earned entries to paid. It asks first.

  18. 18

    Commission report

    One row per job with the commission on it, filtered by salesperson and dates, and exported for payroll. The commissions tour covers each filter.

  19. 19

    Job costing

    Profit on each job.

  20. 20

    Date range

    This month, last month, this year, last year or the last 12 months. The dates in use show next to it.

  21. 21

    Revenue, costs, profit, hours

    Revenue with change orders, job costs (labor at the burn rate, materials, subs), gross profit and margin, and crew hours against the estimate.

  22. 22

    Job profit

    Each job finished in the range, plus those still running: revenue, cost, hours, profit, margin against the estimate. Click a job to open it.

  23. 23

    Jobs, salespeople or crews

    Adds the same numbers up by salesperson or by crew instead of by job. Export downloads every job's details.

  24. 24

    Burn rates

    What one man-hour costs: wages, taxes, comp, benefits, overhead. Owner and office add a New rate from a date. The job costing tour shows the form.

  25. 25

    Timesheets

    Crew hours have their own screen.

  26. 26

    Open Timesheets

    Each person's week from the crew app: every segment, overtime past 40 h, missed clock-outs, corrections, approval and the ADP export.
