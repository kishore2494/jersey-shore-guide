Everything on the Schedule

25 steps Every button
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on the schedule: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    The Schedule board

    Crews down the side, days across, two weeks at a time. Every sold piece of work gets its crew and its days here.

  2. 2

    Move through the weeks

    The arrows go back or forward a week; Today comes back to this week.

  3. 3

    Board or month

    Crews · 2 weeks is this board; Month shows the whole month. We'll look at Month at the end.

  4. 4

    To schedule

    Every sold piece of work without a crew and a start day waits here, approved change orders too.

  5. 5

    Waiting work

    Job colour, work name, customer, job number, town, crew-hours and when it was sold. A red Permit hold tag: it can be booked but can't start yet.

  6. 6

    Schedule it from here

    The calendar button opens the schedule form.

  7. 7

    Crew

    Pick the crew.

  8. 8

    Start

    The first day on site.

  9. 9

    Budgeted crew-hours

    Days = these hours ÷ the crew's hours a day. A preview of the days shows under the form.

  10. 10

    Days

    Or type the number of days yourself (0 = from the hours).

  11. 11

    Book it

    Schedule books it and tells you if the crew goes over capacity on any day. The tour doesn't press it.

  12. 12

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  13. 13

    Or drag it

    Drag a card onto a crew's start day and the form opens there. Or click the card to pick it, like this …

  14. 14

    … then click the day

    … then click the crew's start day on the board: the form opens with that crew and day.

  15. 15

    Put it back

    Click the picked card again to let go of it.

  16. 16

    Appointments row

    How many sales visits and tasks there are each day, and whose. Hover for the times.

  17. 17

    A crew

    Crew colour, name, lead and how many crew-hours it can do in a day.

  18. 18

    Booked work

    One colour per job, the same every day it runs (↳ = continues). Click it to open the job; drag it to another day or crew to move it. A multi-day job moves together.

  19. 19

    How full the day is

    The line under a day is the crew's load against what it can do. Faded blocks are done; work that has started can't be dragged.

  20. 20

    Over capacity

    A red outline: the crew is booked for more hours than it can do that day. Move something to another day or crew.

  21. 21

    What the colours mean

    Bars: one colour per job, faded = done. Each crew's colour and hours a day, the red over-capacity outline, and the lock for a permit hold.

  22. 22

    Month view

    Shows the whole month.

  23. 23

    The month

    Here a colour is a crew (the key is above). Click a bar to open the job; drag it to another day to move it.

  24. 24

    Other months

    The arrows go a month back or forward; This month comes back.

  25. 25

    Back to the board

    Crews · 2 weeks goes back to the crew board.
