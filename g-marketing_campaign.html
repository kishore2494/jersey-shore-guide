Write and send an email campaign

25 steps · 3:22 video Owner, Office

In the office app, press Help and type write and send an email campaign: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 6, Marketing, at 51:16
  1. 1

    Campaigns

    Campaigns are written here.

  2. 2

    Start from a season

    Each card brings the wording, a button and who should get it. New campaign starts blank; Open finishes a draft. The tour opens this one.

  3. 3

    The email

    What the customer reads. The preview on the right changes as you type.

  4. 4

    Campaign name

    Only the office sees it, e.g. Spring cleanup 2027.

  5. 5

    Subject line

    What they see in their inbox. {first_name} becomes each customer's first name.

  6. 6

    The message

    Your own wording. A blank line starts a new paragraph; {first_name}, {company} and {company_phone} are filled in for each customer.

  7. 7

    The button

    The big button in the email, e.g. Book a spring cleanup, and its link: your request form once it is set up.

  8. 8

    Design

    How it looks in the inbox: layout, colour, pictures and headline.

  9. 9

    Layout

    Photo header puts a wide photo of your work on top; Colour banner a coloured band with the headline; Plain is a simple letter.

  10. 10

    Header picture

    Paste a picture link or press Upload. A wide landscape photo, at least 1200 px across, looks best.

  11. 11

    Colour banner

    The tour puts the starter's colour banner back.

  12. 12

    Colour

    Six brand colours for the banner and the button: bay navy, paver red, lawn green, sand gold, slate and violet.

  13. 13

    Headline

    The big words at the top, and a line under them (e.g. Reserve your spot before April).

  14. 14

    A picture in the message

    An optional photo in the body, from a link or Upload. Once it's added, a Caption box appears.

  15. 15

    Who gets it

    Choose the customers. The count at the bottom updates as you go.

  16. 16

    Customers or leads

    Customers, Leads, Both, or Inactive customers.

  17. 17

    Residential or commercial

    Any, Residential or Commercial.

  18. 18

    Towns

    Click towns to send only there; none picked means everywhere. The number is how many customers each town has.

  19. 19

    Narrow it down

    Had a kind of work in the last N months, lost an estimate lately, no work lately, on a maintenance plan or not. Demo customers stay out.

  20. 20

    How many get it

    The count, a few names and a bar by town. Customers who opted out, or have no email, are never included.

  21. 21

    Send a test first

    Type your email and press Send test to see it in a real inbox. It saves the draft first.

  22. 22

    Save draft

    Keeps the campaign as a draft to finish later. The tour doesn't save.

  23. 23

    Send it

    Sends to everyone counted, paced per hour (it asks first, and you can Stop it while it goes out). Greyed out until live email is on.

  24. 24

    What they'll see

    A live preview with a sample customer's name, updated as you type and design.

  25. 25

    Back to campaigns

    Leaves the composer. Nothing is kept unless you pressed Save draft.
