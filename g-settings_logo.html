Put your logo on every estimate, invoice and email

3 steps · 0:36 video

In the office app, press Help and type put your logo on every estimate, invoice and email: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Logo

    Upload your logo, a PNG or JPG with a white or clear background. It goes at the top of every estimate, invoice and email.

  2. 2

    Most days a crew works

    Scheduling warns when a crew would work more days than this in a week, for example a Saturday on top of a full week.

  3. 3

    Save settings

    Keeps the logo and your changes.
