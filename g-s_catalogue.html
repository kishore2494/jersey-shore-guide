Everything on Catalogue

22 steps · 3:22 video Every button

In the office app, press Help and type everything on catalogue: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 15, Catalogue, at 2:06:47
  1. 1

    Catalogue

    Every service, material, equipment item and fee your estimates pick from (Service Autopilot's products). The number under the title counts the active items.

  2. 2

    Add an item

    New item opens a blank form. Only people who see money can add or change items.

  3. 3

    Name

    The name on estimates and in the list. It's the only thing you must fill in.

  4. 4

    Kind

    Service (labor), Material, Equipment or Fee. Only a service can carry a kit of materials. The Kind filter on the list uses it too.

  5. 5

    Category

    Hardscape, Plants, Irrigation… It groups the list. A crew whose kind of work matches the category gets won sections of it first.

  6. 6

    Unit

    What one of it is: ea, sq ft, ton, job. The quantity on an estimate counts these.

  7. 7

    Cost and price

    Price is what the customer pays per unit; Cost, just before it, is what it costs you. The list works out the margin from the two.

  8. 8

    Crew-hours per unit

    How many crew-hours one unit takes. It fills the hours on estimates, and the schedule turns those hours into days for a crew.

  9. 9

    Description

    What the customer reads on the estimate line. It fills in when the item is added to an estimate.

  10. 10

    Taxable

    Ticked: sales tax applies to it. Untick it for items that are never taxed.

  11. 11

    Active

    Active items are offered in estimates. Untick to retire one: estimates that have it keep it, and the list hides it unless Show inactive is ticked.

  12. 12

    Add item

    Add item saves it (prices can't be negative). Cancel, or the X at the top, closes the form without saving.

  13. 13

    Close the form

    The tour closes it without saving anything.

  14. 14

    Search the catalogue

    Type part of a name, an item code or a word from the description; the list narrows as you type.

  15. 15

    Filter by kind

    Show only services, materials, equipment or fees.

  16. 16

    Filter by category

    Show one category. The number beside each category is how many items it has.

  17. 17

    Show inactive

    Tick it to include the items you switched off. They carry an Inactive tag.

  18. 18

    The list

    Each row: name, code and description, category, kind, unit, and Used (how many estimate lines use it). Click a row to change the item. 60 rows a page.

  19. 19

    Cost, price and margin

    Cost, Price and Margin show only for people who see money. A margin in red means the price is below the cost.

  20. 20

    Open an item

    Click a row to open the same form, filled in. Changing a price never changes estimates already written. Here: a service.

  21. 21

    A service's kit

    Its kit: the materials that come with this service on every estimate, hidden from the customer unless ticked. The tour "Set up a service's kit" shows each part.

  22. 22

    Close it

    The tour closes it without saving. That's the whole Catalogue.
