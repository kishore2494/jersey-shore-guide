Set up or change a maintenance plan

24 steps · 2:34 video

In the office app, press Help and type set up or change a maintenance plan: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 9, Maintenance, at 1:25:38
  1. 1

    New plan

    Starts a plan for a customer (a customer's page can start one too).

  2. 2

    Pick the customer

    Type a name, phone or street and click the customer; the plan form opens.

  3. 3

    Close the form

    The x closes it without starting a plan.

  4. 4

    Open a plan

    An existing plan opens in the same form. Let's open one.

  5. 5

    Service

    What the plan is, e.g. Weekly lawn care.

  6. 6

    From the catalogue

    Click a catalogue item to fill in the service name and its price.

  7. 7

    How often

    Weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly, spring + fall, or Snow (on call: add a visit when it snows; pricing is on the Snow screen).

  8. 8

    Crew

    The crew that does the visits.

  9. 9

    Day of the week

    The day the visits fall on, or Any.

  10. 10

    Season

    Season from and to; the strip under it shows the months. No visits outside the season.

  11. 11

    Start and end

    When the plan starts, and an end date if it has one.

  12. 12

    Crew-hours a visit

    How long a visit takes. It counts against the crew's day on the board.

  13. 13

    Price

    The price per visit, or the monthly fee.

  14. 14

    Billing

    Each finished visit (billed at month end), a fixed monthly fee in season, or not billed here.

  15. 15

    Note for the crew

    Gate code, dog, what to skip … It shows on the crew's phone.

  16. 16

    Tell the customer

    Emails the customer the date of the first visit. Handy for cleanups; leave it off for weekly mowing.

  17. 17

    Next visits

    The next dates the plan will book. They change as you change the settings.

  18. 18

    Booked visits

    Visits already on the schedule.

  19. 19

    Move a visit

    Move puts a visit on another day (a rain day).

  20. 20

    Skip a visit

    Skip drops that visit; it stays skipped and won't come back.

  21. 21

    Save the plan

    Save plan (Create plan for a new one) books its visits. The tour doesn't press it.

  22. 22

    Pause

    Pause takes the future visits off; Resume brings the plan back.

  23. 23

    End the plan

    Future visits come off the schedule; finished visits can still be billed. It asks first.

  24. 24

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.
