Everything on Deliveries

25 steps Every button
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on deliveries: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Deliveries

    The drivers' week (instead of the Google Calendar and the whiteboard) and every dumpster: where it is and who has it.

  2. 2

    Pick the week

    The arrows go back or forward a week.

  3. 3

    New run

    Books a material delivery, or a container drop, pickup, swap or move. Tour: "Book a delivery or container run".

  4. 4

    Runs today

    Runs still to do today, and which drivers have them.

  5. 5

    This week

    All the runs this week and how many are done.

  6. 6

    Containers out

    Containers at customers, out of all of them; the rest are in the yard.

  7. 7

    Full or late

    Containers the customer says are full, waiting for pickup. With none, it counts late runs: earlier days not done, also listed in a red banner.

  8. 8

    The drivers' week

    A row per driver, a column per day (Sunday only when a run is on it). Not assigned holds runs with no driver yet.

  9. 9

    A run

    Time, container number, customer, and what it is with the truck. The edge colour is the kind of run; done runs are faded with a tick.

  10. 10

    Drag to move

    Drag a run to another day or driver to move it; the driver's phone follows. Done runs stay put.

  11. 11

    Add a run on a day

    The + in a day starts a run for that driver and day.

  12. 12

    Open a run

    Click a run to see all of it.

  13. 13

    The details

    What, when, driver, truck, customer, address, container, job, load and notes; once done, who did it and when.

  14. 14

    Edit

    Changes the run (the same form as New run).

  15. 15

    Mark done

    For when the driver couldn't on the phone. A drop asks which container went out; the container moves with the run.

  16. 16

    Cancel run

    Cancels the run (it asks first).

  17. 17

    Customer

    Opens the customer.

  18. 18

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  19. 19

    Container yard

    Every dumpster / roll-off: its number, size, where it is and what's next. Tour: "Track dumpsters".

  20. 20

    Out

    Containers at customers, full ones too.

  21. 21

    Yard

    Ready to go.

  22. 22

    Repair

    Out of service.

  23. 23

    All

    Back to all of them.

  24. 24

    Add container

    A new dumpster: its number, size and notes.

  25. 25

    A container

    Size, number, status (In the yard, Out, Full · pickup due, Repair), customer and address, days out and the next run. Click it for history and actions.
