Book a delivery or container run

17 steps · 1:58 video

In the office app, press Help and type book a delivery or container run: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    New run

    Opens the run form.

  2. 2

    What kind of run

    Material delivery, drop a container, pick one up, swap (dump and bring it back), move one, or any other run.

  3. 3

    Day

    The day of the run.

  4. 4

    Time

    The start time.

  5. 5

    Takes

    How long it keeps the driver busy: 30 minutes up to all day.

  6. 6

    Driver

    Only drivers are listed. Leave it empty and the run goes under Not assigned.

  7. 7

    Customer

    Search by name, phone or street and pick them from the list; the address becomes theirs.

  8. 8

    Address

    Only if it is not the customer's address: a job site, a second property …

  9. 9

    A container run

    For a drop, pickup, swap or move the form asks which container.

  10. 10

    Container

    For a drop: one from the yard, or leave it for the driver. For a pickup, swap or move: the one at the customer.

  11. 11

    Truck

    Which truck (from Fleet).

  12. 12

    Load / what for

    For a delivery, the load (6 pallets pavers, 22 t QP). For a container, what it is for.

  13. 13

    Notes for the driver

    Gate code, where to put it … The driver sees it on the phone.

  14. 14

    Book it

    Schedule run saves it and the driver sees it on the phone. You are warned if the truck or container is busy. The tour doesn't press it.

  15. 15

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  16. 16

    Quicker: + on a day

    The + in a driver's day opens the same form for that day and driver.

  17. 17

    From a container

    A container's card books runs too: Schedule pickup or Swap for one that is out, Drop it at a customer for one in the yard.
