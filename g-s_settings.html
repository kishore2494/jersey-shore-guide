Everything on Settings

25 steps · 3:40 video Every buttonOwner, Office

In the office app, press Help and type everything on settings: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 19, Settings, at 2:33:48
  1. 1

    Settings

    Company details, tax, contract terms, payroll, fleet, payment schedules, commission and the text on documents. Only the owner and the office open it.

  2. 2

    Save settings

    Nothing here is kept until you press it (payment schedules have their own Save). It checks the numbers first.

  3. 3

    Company

    Company name, phone, email, office address, licence line and time zone, printed on estimates, invoices and emails.

  4. 4

    Estimates

    How long estimates are valid, the days to the first and second follow-up, and the amount above which a contract needs a signature.

  5. 5

    Sales tax

    The NJ sales tax rate added to taxable work.

  6. 6

    Labour cost per crew-hour

    What a crew-hour costs you, for the margins on estimates. Job costing uses the burn rates under Reports.

  7. 7

    How commission works

    Commission on the job's gross profit or on the sale; earned when the whole job is paid, or on each paid invoice (sale only).

  8. 8

    Card surcharge

    The % added to card payments, shown on invoices and contracts. Blank: no surcharge, and it isn't mentioned.

  9. 9

    Extras rate

    The rate per man-hour for extra work not in the estimate, printed in the contract terms.

  10. 10

    Late charge

    A % a month added to late invoices once a month, once they are late by the days below. Blank = off.

  11. 11

    Payroll

    Overtime after how many hours a week, and the ADP company code for the pay-data file from Timesheets.

  12. 12

    Customer notices

    Which types of work (crew specializations) get the "you're on the schedule for …" email. Each job can override it.

  13. 13

    Fleet manager

    Who gets the Monday due list and the crews' truck reports.

  14. 14

    When "due soon" starts

    How close oil and tires (miles), inspections (days) and machine service (engine hours) must be to count as due soon.

  15. 15

    Snow

    The units for salt and ice melt, and whether snow work is taxed.

  16. 16

    Payment schedules

    The payments an estimate is split into (each picks one, by type of work). Each card shows them and what they come to on a $20,000 job.

  17. 17

    A schedule

    Click one to change it. The top line says whether it's the default or which work it is for.

  18. 18

    New schedule

    Makes another one. The tour "Set up a payment schedule" shows the editor.

  19. 19

    Commission rates

    The rate for everyone, plus any salesperson with their own rate.

  20. 20

    Add a salesperson rate

    Adds a row: pick the salesperson and their %. The x removes one. Kept with Save settings.

  21. 21

    Text on documents

    The standard terms printed on estimates, and the footer on invoices.

  22. 22

    Open Automations

    Follow-ups, reminders, texts and the sending mailbox are set up under Automations, with the wording of every message.

  23. 23

    Live email

    Off: every email goes to the test address instead of the customer. Turn it on when ready; it needs the company email. Kept with Save settings.

  24. 24

    Test address

    Where emails go while live email is off.

  25. 25

    Customer quote link

    Optional: a link customers can open; {token} is replaced for each estimate.
