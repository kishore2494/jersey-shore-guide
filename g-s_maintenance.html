Everything on Maintenance

22 steps · 2:36 video Every button

In the office app, press Help and type everything on maintenance: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 9, Maintenance, at 1:23:08
  1. 1

    Maintenance

    Recurring work: lawn care, beds, spring and fall cleanups, snow. Plans put their own visits on the crew's schedule weeks ahead.

  2. 2

    The numbers

    Active plans and visits this week. People who see money also see the yearly value and what is ready to bill.

  3. 3

    Yearly increase

    Owner and office: raise every plan's price from a date and email each customer. Tour: "Raise maintenance prices for the year".

  4. 4

    New plan

    Starts a plan; first pick the customer.

  5. 5

    Find the customer

    Type a name, phone or street and click them; the plan form opens. Tour: "Set up or change a maintenance plan".

  6. 6

    Close the form

    The x closes it without starting a plan.

  7. 7

    Plans

    Every plan; the number on each tab is how many. Visits and Billing are next to it.

  8. 8

    Customer

    Who the plan is for, and where.

  9. 9

    Service and how often

    Weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly, spring + fall or snow (on call), and the day of the week.

  10. 10

    Season

    The months it runs are green; the mark is this month. Visits only go on the schedule in season.

  11. 11

    Crew

    The crew that does the visits. With No crew, no visits get booked.

  12. 12

    Price

    Per visit, a month (fixed monthly fee), or not billed here.

  13. 13

    Next visit

    The next booked visit and how many are done this year, or on call, needs a crew, out of season.

  14. 14

    Status

    Active, Paused or Ended.

  15. 15

    Open a plan

    Click a plan to change it, move or skip a visit, pause or end it.

  16. 16

    Visits

    This week and next, day by day.

  17. 17

    The visit board

    Each visit is coloured by its crew; faded = done. Click a visit to open the customer.

  18. 18

    Billing

    Month-end billing for maintenance.

  19. 19

    Bill up to

    Finished visits and monthly fees up to this date. Change it and the list updates.

  20. 20

    Who gets billed

    One draft invoice per customer: a line per finished visit, or the monthly fee for each month in season.

  21. 21

    Make the invoices

    Creates the draft invoices; check and send them from Invoices. The tour doesn't press it.

  22. 22

    Back to the plans

    Plans is where most of the work is.
