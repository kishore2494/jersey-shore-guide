Invoice a job's payment schedule

15 steps · 1:56 video Money

In the office app, press Help and type invoice a job's payment schedule: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Open the job

    The payment schedule is on the job. Let's open one.

  2. 2

    Payments

    The payment schedule from the contract. Invoice each payment in one click when it is due.

  3. 3

    The totals

    Contract (with change orders), invoiced, paid and remaining, then the next payment due and when.

  4. 4

    Each payment

    A tick once paid, then the name, the %, when it is due, Invoiced or Not invoiced yet, and the amount.

  5. 5

    Open its invoice

    Once a payment is invoiced, this opens the invoice to send it or record a payment.

  6. 6

    Invoice a payment

    Makes a draft invoice for that payment and opens it to check and send. Once the job is complete, "Invoice the balance" bills what is left.

  7. 7

    Change the schedule

    Edit schedule re-plans the payments still to bill (owner and office).

  8. 8

    Invoiced ones are locked

    Payments already invoiced stay as they are. The ones below share what is still to bill.

  9. 9

    Start from a preset

    One click fills a common schedule; then adjust it.

  10. 10

    One payment

    Its name; the arrows move it up or down and x removes it. Then % of the rest, a fixed $ or the balance, and when it is due.

  11. 11

    Add a payment

    Adds another payment.

  12. 12

    Save it

    Checks it adds up to what is left, then saves. The tour doesn't press it.

  13. 13

    Leave it as it was

    Cancel closes the editor.

  14. 14

    Invoices on this job

    Every invoice for this job with its status. Click one to open it.

  15. 15

    Also in the job folder

    The folder lists each payment again with how it was paid, for the paper file.
