Design an email template with AI (or a ready layout)

11 steps · 1:54 video

In the office app, press Help and type design an email template with ai: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 6, Marketing, at 54:30
  1. 1

    Design an email with AI

    Opens the email designer. You describe the email and add photos; the design builds on the left.

  2. 2

    The email, live

    Exactly what customers will get: the design, your words, and our footer with the unsubscribe line. Computer and Phone switch the width.

  3. 3

    Say what you want

    What it's for, the feel, the colours and what to show. For example: a spring cleanup email for our regular customers, my patio photo across the top, fresh greens.

  4. 4

    Ideas to start from

    Tap one to fill the box, then change it to suit.

  5. 5

    Attach your photos

    Up to six photos of your work. Write what each one shows, so the design puts the right one on top.

  6. 6

    Main colour

    Pick one of your colours, or let the design choose.

  7. 7

    Design it

    The AI makes the design and its words. Then ask for any change, like a bigger headline; each change is a version you can go back to. AI has to be switched on for your account; until then, the ready layouts below do the same job.

  8. 8

    Or pick a ready layout

    Four proven designs that take your photos, colour and words. Click Photo on top.

  9. 9

    The words

    Subject, headline, the line under it, the message and the button. Every campaign made from this template starts with these, and can change them.

  10. 10

    Name it

    A name you'll recognise, like Spring photo hero.

  11. 11

    Save as template

    Keeps it in Email templates, ready for any campaign. The tour doesn't save anything: press it yourself when it's right.
