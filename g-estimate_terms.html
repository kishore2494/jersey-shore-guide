Payment schedule, tax and ST-8 on an estimate

11 steps Money

In the office app, press Help and type payment schedule, tax and st-8 on an estimate: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Open the estimate

    Terms are set on each estimate. The tour opens this one.

  2. 2

    Contract terms

    What the customer signs up to if they accept: the payment schedule and whether sales tax applies.

  3. 3

    Pick the payment schedule

    The schedules from Settings; the default for this kind of work is already picked. Choosing one saves it on the estimate.

  4. 4

    Each payment, worked out

    Deposit, start of work, completion… with what each comes to and when it's due. They follow the total as you edit.

  5. 5

    A custom split

    "Custom for this estimate…" opens an editor: start from 30/60/10, 50/50 or deposit + final; set %, fixed $ or the balance, and when each is due.

  6. 6

    NJ ST-8: no sales tax

    Switch on for new construction (patio, wall, driveway): no sales tax, and the customer signs an ST-8 to keep on the job. It takes effect at once.

  7. 7

    Terms added from Settings

    The standard terms from Settings that print on this estimate. Click the line to read them.

  8. 8

    Tax in the totals

    Sales tax is the estimate's Tax % on the taxable sections only. Tax exempt sections, and ST-8 jobs, pay none.

  9. 9

    Tax %

    The sales tax rate for this estimate. Change it only for a special case.

  10. 10

    Valid until

    How long the price holds. It prints on the estimate.

  11. 11

    Terms for this estimate

    Extra terms for this estimate only, printed with it. Press Save to keep them.
