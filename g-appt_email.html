Email an appointment to someone

2 steps · 0:31 video

In the office app, press Help and type email an appointment to someone: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 4, Appointments, at 22:00
  1. 1

    Open an appointment

    Click any appointment.

  2. 2

    Email it

    Sends the details to anyone: when, the customer, their phone and address, and the notes. Click it and a small form opens: the address to send it to, a note from you if you like (Can you cover this one?), and Send. It is logged on the appointment.
