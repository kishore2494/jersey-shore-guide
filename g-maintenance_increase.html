Raise maintenance prices for the year

7 steps · 0:57 video MoneyOwner, Office

In the office app, press Help and type raise maintenance prices for the year: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 9, Maintenance, at 1:28:05
  1. 1

    Yearly increase

    Raises every maintenance plan due its increase, in one go.

  2. 2

    Increase

    The % to add. Leave it blank to use each plan's own % (5% unless set).

  3. 3

    Starting

    From this date visits not billed yet take the new price.

  4. 4

    Tell the customers

    Emails each customer who has an email their new price.

  5. 5

    Which plans

    Each plan due: last increase, price now, new price, %. Untick any to skip. Per-storm snow plans are left out.

  6. 6

    Apply it

    Apply the increase shows the yearly value before and after and asks first. The tour doesn't press it.

  7. 7

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.
