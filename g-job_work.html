Schedule, start and complete work on a job

27 steps

In the office app, press Help and type schedule, start and complete work on a job: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Open the job

    Work items live on the job. Let's open one.

  2. 2

    Work items

    One item per estimate section and per approved change order, each with its own crew, crew-hours and days.

  3. 3

    Add work

    For extra work that is not on the estimate.

  4. 4

    What

    Say what the work is, e.g. Extra steps.

  5. 5

    Crew-hours

    The hours the crew needs in total. The board turns them into days using the crew's hours a day.

  6. 6

    Crew

    Who does it. You can leave it and pick the crew when you schedule it.

  7. 7

    Add it

    Add puts it on the job and in the "To schedule" list on the board. The tour doesn't press it.

  8. 8

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  9. 9

    One work item

    Crew colour, the name, work order number, crew, crew-hours, hours the crew logged and photos, then its status.

  10. 10

    Its days

    The days it is booked. A red "over" day means the crew has more hours than it can do that day: move this or the other work.

  11. 11

    Reopen

    On a finished item: puts it back in progress when the crew has to go back.

  12. 12

    Complete

    Marks the item done. When the last item is done the job turns Complete, ready to invoice. The crew lead can also do it on the phone.

  13. 13

    Materials

    Click Materials to see what this item needs. A tick means the crew loaded it.

  14. 14

    Reschedule

    Opens the schedule form for this item. On an item not on the board yet the button says Schedule.

  15. 15

    Crew

    Pick the crew; each shows its speciality.

  16. 16

    Start day

    The first day on site.

  17. 17

    Budgeted crew-hours

    The hours budgeted. The days are these hours ÷ the crew's hours a day.

  18. 18

    Or set the days

    Leave 0 to work the days out from the hours, or type the number of days.

  19. 19

    The days it takes

    A preview of the days. Weekends are skipped unless the job works them (Working days card).

  20. 20

    Take it off the board

    Puts the item back in the To schedule list.

  21. 21

    Book the days

    Schedule books it and tells you if the crew goes over capacity on any day. The tour doesn't press it.

  22. 22

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  23. 23

    Not on the board yet

    An item with no days says "Not on the schedule yet" and has a Schedule button: the same form.

  24. 24

    Remove an item

    The bin removes an item that has not started (it asks first). It comes off the schedule too.

  25. 25

    Crew note

    A note for the crew on this item, from the estimate or a change order. It shows on their phone.

  26. 26

    Open the job

    Now a job that is booked but not started, to see Start.

  27. 27

    Start

    Marks the item in progress (the crew lead usually does it on the phone). A job on Permit hold shows a red tag instead and can't start.
