Everything on Marketing

25 steps Every button
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on marketing: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Marketing

    Seasonal emails to the right customers, your Spring and Fall cleanup sign-ups, and requests from your website, on one screen.

  2. 2

    The numbers

    Customers you can reach by email, how many opted out, campaigns sent, cleanup sign-ups waiting and new website requests.

  3. 3

    New campaign

    Owner and office: starts a blank campaign. Most start from a season card instead. "Write and send an email campaign" walks through it.

  4. 4

    Live email is off

    While this shows, you can write and test campaigns but nothing goes to customers. Live email is switched on in Automations → Sending setup.

  5. 5

    Campaigns

    Every campaign you've written, and the season starters.

  6. 6

    A campaign

    Name, status (Draft, Scheduled, Sending, Sent, Stopped), subject, who made it and when. While sending, a bar shows how many went, wait or failed.

  7. 7

    Open a draft

    Opens a draft to finish it. While one is going out, Stop is here instead: emails already sent stay sent, the rest are cancelled.

  8. 8

    Duplicate

    Copies a campaign as a new draft: last fall's email, ready for this fall.

  9. 9

    Delete a draft

    Deletes a draft you don't need (it asks first). Only drafts can be deleted.

  10. 10

    Start from a season

    Spring or fall cleanup, paver sealing, next year's project, "still thinking it over?" and maintenance plans: each brings its wording and who should get it.

  11. 11

    Cleanup sign-ups

    Your Spring and Fall Service Request Forms and everyone who signed up, in the order received.

  12. 12

    Your forms

    One card per season with the sign-ups waiting. The link for your website and emails, with Copy link, appears here once the online forms are on.

  13. 13

    See the form

    Shows the form question for question, as customers see it. The tour opens it.

  14. 14

    What customers fill in

    Name, phone, address, the services they want, meet a manager or just a quote, the date they'd like, and any billing or contact changes.

  15. 15

    Close it

    The x closes the preview.

  16. 16

    Someone calls instead

    Take a phone sign-up asks the same questions and puts them in line. "Take a cleanup sign-up on the phone" shows the form.

  17. 17

    Spring or Fall

    Switches the list below between the Spring and the Fall sign-ups.

  18. 18

    In the order received

    Number in line, when and how it came in, the customer, what they want, estimate style, preferred date, and flags: auto-pay call, billing change, note.

  19. 19

    Accept

    Makes the visit: "meet a manager" becomes an estimate visit to book, "just send a quote" a stop-by. New people are added as leads.

  20. 20

    Dismiss

    The x takes a sign-up off the list (a duplicate, a test).

  21. 21

    Website requests

    Requests from the form on your website and from campaign buttons.

  22. 22

    The request form

    Its link, for your website and campaign buttons, with Copy link. It shows once the public form is switched on.

  23. 23

    A new request

    Name, town, phone, email, the services they want, their message and the best time. Ones that look like spam are flagged.

  24. 24

    Accept

    Matches an existing customer or adds a new lead, makes a ticket for the visit, and opens the customer.

  25. 25

    Dismiss

    Spam or not for you. Accepted and dismissed requests move to a Handled list below, with who handled them.
