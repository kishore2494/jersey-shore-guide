Guide / Setup

Everything on Automations

25 steps Every buttonOwner, Office
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on automations: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Automations

    What the system does without anyone pressing a button: follow-ups, reminders and notices. Every message waits in the queue first, where you can stop it.

  2. 2

    At a glance

    How many of the 9 automations are switched on, messages sent today and in the last 30 days, and how many are waiting to go.

  3. 3

    Run now

    Runs the checks straight away instead of waiting for the next run: whatever is due gets queued and its call tickets made. A message says what it did.

  4. 4

    Wording

    Opens the Wording tab: the words of every email and text.

  5. 5

    Today's clock

    Each dot is a message sent today, at the hour it went. The red hand is now.

  6. 6

    Engine

    Green when the automatic run is connected (every 10 minutes). Until then the checks run whenever the office opens the app.

  7. 7

    Last run

    When the checks last ran. Amber if it was over an hour ago.

  8. 8

    Sending

    When the last message went out. Amber if a message has waited more than 20 minutes.

  9. 9

    Live email

    On: messages go to customers. Off: they go to the test address. Change it under Sending setup.

  10. 10

    Texts

    Whether text messages are switched on (Sending setup).

  11. 11

    Online estimates

    Whether the online estimate links, where customers view and accept online, are installed.

  12. 12

    What runs

    Every automation, with its switch and timing.

  13. 13

    One automation

    What it does, its flow (what starts it, the wait, what goes out), its timing, how many went out in 30 days and Edit wording. Change a number and a Save button appears.

  14. 14

    On or off

    The switch turns it on or off and saves straight away. The tour "Switch reminders and notices on or off" goes through all nine.

  15. 15

    Queue

    Messages waiting to go.

  16. 16

    Waiting to go

    Each message, customer, subject and address, and when it goes. The eye shows it, the arrow sends it now, the x cancels it. If its reason goes away (paid, answered) it cancels itself.

  17. 17

    Sent

    What went out.

  18. 18

    What went out

    The last 80 messages, newest first, with All / Sent / Not sent. The eye shows a message; the round arrow sends a failed, cancelled or skipped one again.

  19. 19

    Wording

    The words of every message.

  20. 20

    The words

    Pick a message under Every message, change its subject and text, and see it on the right as a customer gets it. The tour "Change the wording of an email or text" shows each part.

  21. 21

    Sending setup

    The Sending setup tab: how messages are sent.

  22. 22

    How sending is set up

    Live email, the test address, the address customers write to, texts, links in messages, crew photo rules and QuickBooks items. Save setup keeps them.

  23. 23

    Runs

    The Runs tab: every run of the checks.

  24. 24

    Automation runs

    When each run happened, what started it (the timer, the office or Run now), how long it took and what it did. A red tag means a part failed.

  25. 25

    Online estimate events

    Online estimates opened, accepted and declined, as the platform reported them. Click one to open the estimate.
