Track dumpsters: drop, pick up, swap

24 steps · 2:10 video

In the office app, press Help and type track dumpsters: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 10, Deliveries, at 1:33:38
  1. 1

    Container yard

    Every dumpster / roll-off, where it is and what is next.

  2. 2

    Filter

    All, Out, Yard or Repair, with how many are in each.

  3. 3

    Add container

    Adds a new dumpster.

  4. 4

    Number

    Its own number, e.g. D-18.

  5. 5

    Size

    10, 15, 20, 30 or 40 yards.

  6. 6

    Notes

    Anything worth knowing about it.

  7. 7

    Save it

    Save adds it to the yard. The tour doesn't press it.

  8. 8

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  9. 9

    One that is out

    Click it to see where it is and what it has done.

  10. 10

    Where it is

    Status, size, customer, address, job, out since (and how many days) and the next run.

  11. 11

    History

    Every drop, pickup, swap, move and repair, with who and when.

  12. 12

    Schedule pickup

    Books a pickup run with the customer and address filled in.

  13. 13

    Swap

    Books a swap: dump it and bring it back.

  14. 14

    Customer says full

    Marks it Full · pickup due; it shows on the Full box at the top.

  15. 15

    Edit

    Change the number, size or notes. Owner and office can remove one that is in the yard.

  16. 16

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  17. 17

    One in the yard

    Ready to go.

  18. 18

    Drop it at a customer

    Books a drop run with this container.

  19. 19

    Needs repair

    Takes it out of service.

  20. 20

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  21. 21

    One in repair

    Out of service.

  22. 22

    Back in service

    Puts it back in the yard, ready to go.

  23. 23

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  24. 24

    Full · pickup due

    The customer says it is full. Book its pickup or a swap from its card.
