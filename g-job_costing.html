Check a job's profit and enter its costs

21 steps · 2:46 video Money

In the office app, press Help and type check a job's profit and enter its costs: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Open the job

    Profit and costs are on the job. Let's open one.

  2. 2

    Job costing

    Running numbers while the job is on, final numbers once it is complete. They move as the crew logs time and costs come in.

  3. 3

    Margin and profit

    The ring is the margin. Beside it: gross profit so far (or the estimated profit before work starts), the revenue with change orders, and the estimated margin.

  4. 4

    Where the money goes

    The top bar is actual: labor, materials, subs, other, and what is left as profit. The bar under it is the estimate. The colour key is below.

  5. 5

    Estimate vs actual

    Crew hours, labor, materials, subcontractors, other and total cost: estimated, actual and the difference (red = over the estimate).

  6. 6

    By work item

    Opens the same comparison for each work item; change orders are marked CO.

  7. 7

    Profit report

    Opens Reports → Job costing with every job side by side. The burn rates that price labor are set there.

  8. 8

    Costs

    Everything bought for this job. Add bills as they come in and the profit above updates.

  9. 9

    Add a cost

    Opens the cost form.

  10. 10

    Kind

    Materials, Subcontractor, Equipment, Dump / disposal, Permit or Other.

  11. 11

    Vendor

    Who you paid, e.g. Cedar Bridge Landscape Supply.

  12. 12

    Amount

    What it cost. A credit can go in as a minus amount.

  13. 13

    Their invoice # and date

    The vendor's invoice number (you are warned if it is on this job twice) and the date beside it.

  14. 14

    For (optional)

    Leave it on the whole job, or charge it to one work item or an approved change order.

  15. 15

    Description

    What it was, e.g. 12 pallets Techo-Bloc, 4 t stone dust.

  16. 16

    Attach receipt

    Attach a photo or PDF of the bill.

  17. 17

    Save the cost

    Add cost saves it against the job. The tour doesn't press it.

  18. 18

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  19. 19

    Change a cost

    The pencil opens a cost to correct it.

  20. 20

    Remove a cost

    The bin takes it off the job's costs (it asks first).

  21. 21

    Crew time by person

    Each day, each person: hours on site + their share of yard and travel time, and what it cost.
