Price snow by snowfall or by the hour

12 steps · 1:28 video Money

In the office app, press Help and type price snow by snowfall or by the hour: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 11, Snow, at 1:38:22
  1. 1

    Open the pricing

    The $ next to a snow plan.

  2. 2

    Two ways to bill

    By snowfall: a price per storm by how much fell. By the hour: men × hours, plus truck, loader, salt and ice melt.

  3. 3

    Labor per man-hour

    Each visit bills men × hours at this rate.

  4. 4

    Truck and loader

    The plow truck and loader rates per hour.

  5. 5

    Salt, ice melt, walks

    Salt per ton and ice melt per bag (by what was used), and a price per visit for walks.

  6. 6

    By snowfall

    Switch to snowfall pricing.

  7. 7

    Snowfall bands

    A price per storm for each band (0" to 1.99", 2" to 3.99" …). The x removes a band.

  8. 8

    Add a band

    Adds the next band.

  9. 9

    Each inch over

    Above the last band, this is added for every started inch.

  10. 10

    Salt, calcium, walks

    A price each time you salt or put down calcium, and per visit for walks (blank if included).

  11. 11

    Save it

    Save pricing keeps it; visits on this plan can then be billed. The tour doesn't press it.

  12. 12

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.
