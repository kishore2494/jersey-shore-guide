Change the wording of an email or text

14 steps · 1:50 video Owner, Office

In the office app, press Help and type change the wording of an email or text: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Start from a card

    Every automation card has its own wording (the Wording button at the top opens the same place).

  2. 2

    Edit wording

    Opens that message. Here: the appointment reminder.

  3. 3

    The message

    Its name, and when it was last changed and by whom ("Standard wording" if never).

  4. 4

    Send it

    Switches this one message off without stopping its automation. Kept when you press Save wording.

  5. 5

    Subject

    The email's subject line. {placeholders} work here too.

  6. 6

    Message

    The email text. A blank line starts a new paragraph. The preview follows as you type.

  7. 7

    Placeholders

    Click one to put it where the cursor is: first name, appointment day and time, who with, address… Each customer gets their own.

  8. 8

    Text message version

    The text version, sent only when texts are switched on. Keep it under 160 characters; the hint counts them.

  9. 9

    Preview

    Below: the message as a real customer would get it, with sample numbers. It follows your changes as you type.

  10. 10

    Save wording

    Keeps it; the next messages use it.

  11. 11

    Undo changes

    Goes back to the saved wording.

  12. 12

    Every message

    Every message the system sends, including some with no switch of their own (You're on the schedule, the yearly maintenance price change). "Off" marks one switched off.

  13. 13

    Pick another one

    Click a message to change it.

  14. 14

    Button

    A message with a link has a button; this is its wording, e.g. View your estimate.
