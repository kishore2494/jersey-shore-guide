Everything on My day

28 steps · 4:58 video Every button

In the office app, press Help and type everything on my day: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 2, My day, at 1:34
  1. 1

    My day: where every morning starts

    Today's date, how many appointments you have today, anything overdue and how many crews are on site. Everything on this screen is about you and today.

  2. 2

    Search everything

    Type a name, phone, street, or an EST-, JOB- or INV- number and pick the result to open it. ⌘K (Ctrl+K) jumps here from any screen.

  3. 3

    Help

    Ask how to do something in your own words. It finds the tour that fits and shows you on the screen, like this one.

  4. 4

    Notifications

    New assignments, wins and payments for you. The red number counts the unread ones. Click one to open it; Mark all read clears them.

  5. 5

    Crews out today

    How many crews are on a job today, out of all your crews. If you see money, this month's wins and the value of estimates out with customers sit beside it.

  6. 6

    New customer

    Opens the New customer form right here: name, phones, address, salesperson, and the first appointment if you like. Saving opens the new customer.

  7. 7

    New appointment

    Book a visit, a phone call or a task for anyone. It goes straight onto that person's day. The Appointments tours walk every field.

  8. 8

    Schedule

    Opens the Schedule board: crews down the side, days across, and the sold work waiting for a date.

  9. 9

    Estimates out

    Sent estimates still waiting on the customer: the value, or the count if you don't see money. Click the box to open Estimates.

  10. 10

    Won this month

    Estimates won since the 1st of the month and how many jobs they made. Click the box to open Jobs.

  11. 11

    To schedule

    Pieces of sold work with no crew or start day yet. Click the box to open the Schedule and give them a date.

  12. 12

    Overdue invoices

    What customers owe past the due date, and on how many invoices. Click the box to open Invoices. People who don't see money get Crews out today here.

  13. 13

    My day

    Your appointments and tasks for today, with anything overdue at the top in red. Add books a new one for you or for someone else.

  14. 14

    Open an appointment

    Each line shows the time, what it's for, the customer, place and phone, and the type. A camera means the visit sent notes or photos. Click a line to open it.

  15. 15

    The appointment

    When, with whom, the customer (click the name to open them), phone, address and office notes. Below: notes, measurements and photos saved on the visit from the phone.

  16. 16

    Edit, visit done, mark done

    Edit changes it. Visit done · estimate pending keeps an estimate visit open as the reminder to write the quote. Mark done closes it. The tour presses none of them.

  17. 17

    Close it

    The X closes the appointment without changing anything.

  18. 18

    Edit from the list

    The pencil opens the appointment's form straight away, to change the day, time, person or details.

  19. 19

    Mark it done

    The tick marks it done and takes it off your day. Use it once the visit, call or task has happened.

  20. 20

    Next 7 days

    Your appointments for the coming week, day by day, so you can plan office time. The lines work the same as in My day.

  21. 21

    What needs you

    Cards here appear only when there is something to do: New from the website, Waiting on a permit (the township hasn't approved yet) and Waiting on a quote (visit done, no estimate).

  22. 22

    New from the website

    Website requests waiting for someone. Accept makes the lead (or finds the existing customer) plus a ticket, and opens them. All opens Marketing.

  23. 23

    Follow-ups

    Sent estimates with no answer yet: click one to open it; Sent or Called records the follow-up. When none are due it says the automatic follow-ups are handling them.

  24. 24

    On site today

    Which crew is on which job today, with the customer, place and "day 3 of 5" on longer jobs. Click a line to open the job.

  25. 25

    Automations

    Messages the automations sent today, how many are waiting to go and any that failed (red). Click it to open Automations.

  26. 26

    Missed clock-outs

    Shows up when someone on a crew forgot to clock out. Fix it on Timesheets before payroll: click it to go there.

  27. 27

    Fleet

    Truck problems the crews reported from the phone, and trucks overdue for service. Click it to open Fleet.

  28. 28

    Latest across the business

    The eight latest things that happened across all customers. Click a name to open that customer.
