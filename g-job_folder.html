Keep the job folder, permit and terms

24 steps · 3:08 video

In the office app, press Help and type keep the job folder, permit and terms: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

▶ Also in the complete walkthrough: chapter 7, Jobs, at 1:15:11
  1. 1

    Open the job

    The folder is on the job. Let's open one.

  2. 2

    The job folder

    The paper cover sheet, kept up to date by itself from the contract, payments and invoices. You tick the rest here.

  3. 3

    Filled in by itself

    Start date, contract emailed, signed contract, the ST-8, each payment and the final bill tick themselves as they happen.

  4. 4

    Ticked by you

    Account tagged, the permit and the final inspection are filled in with the pencil (Edit the folder).

  5. 5

    Contract and payments

    The contract plus change orders, then each payment: paid (and how), part paid or not paid yet.

  6. 6

    Print the cover sheet

    Prints it like the paper one, for the job file.

  7. 7

    Edit the folder

    Dates the system can't know go in here.

  8. 8

    Account tagged

    Tick it once the account is tagged.

  9. 9

    Permit needed

    Then the job can't start until the permit is approved: a red Permit hold banner on the job (Mark the permit approved) and a lock on the board.

  10. 10

    Time and materials

    Bills the crew's on-site man-hours. A "Bill tracked time" card then shows: enter the rate per man-hour, then Make the invoice (a line per day).

  11. 11

    Emailed contract on

    Blank = the day the estimate went out. Type a date if the contract went another day.

  12. 12

    Permit #

    The township's permit number.

  13. 13

    Permit fee

    Goes on the final invoice, or press Bill now on the folder to bill it on its own.

  14. 14

    Permit status

    To apply for, Applied, Approved, Inspection booked, Passed, Failed inspection or Not needed.

  15. 15

    Municipality

    Long Beach Twp, Stafford, Ship Bottom …

  16. 16

    Permit dates

    Applied for on, Approved on, and the next inspection.

  17. 17

    Inspection notes

    What the inspector said, or what is booked.

  18. 18

    Final inspection

    Its date and your initials. A complete permit job without one shows "Call for the final inspection" on the folder.

  19. 19

    Notes

    Anything else for the folder.

  20. 20

    Save the folder

    Save folder keeps it. The tour doesn't press it.

  21. 21

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  22. 22

    Contract terms

    The payment schedule in words. On a capital improvement an ST-8 box shows: no sales tax, so upload the customer's signed NJ ST-8 there.

  23. 23

    Tell the customer their date

    Auto follows the type of work. On or Off sets it for this job: On sends a "you're on the schedule" email when the date is set or moves. It saves at once.

  24. 24

    Work weekends?

    Switch on Saturdays or Sundays for this job. It saves at once and the scheduled days move to match.
