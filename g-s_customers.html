Everything on Customers

25 steps Every button
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on customers: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Customers

    Every customer and lead in one list. The line under the title counts your customers and your leads.

  2. 2

    Export

    Saves the list as it is filtered right now as a file for Excel: code, name, phone, email, address, stage, rep and source. Handy for mailings.

  3. 3

    New customer

    Opens the New customer form above the list. Let's look at it; the tour doesn't save anything.

  4. 4

    Name

    First and last name, or a Company for businesses (leave Company blank for homeowners). Only a name is required.

  5. 5

    Phones and email

    Cell phone, another phone and email. Search finds the customer by any of them later.

  6. 6

    Property address

    Where the work is: street and number.

  7. 7

    The zip fills the town

    Type the 5-digit zip and Town and State fill themselves. You can still change them.

  8. 8

    Billing address

    Ticked, invoices go to the property. Untick it to add a different Bill to name and billing address.

  9. 9

    Source, salesperson, CSR

    Where the lead came from and who referred them, the Salesperson who owns them and the CSR in the office who looks after them.

  10. 10

    Stage, type, invoicing, terms

    Lead, Customer or Inactive; Residential or Commercial; invoiced when work is done or monthly (month-end run); Due on receipt or Net 30.

  11. 11

    Payment and flags

    Check or card, the card's last 4 digits only, Do not market (no seasonal emails) and Tax exempt.

  12. 12

    Office notes

    Gate codes, dogs, best time to call. They show on the customer's screen.

  13. 13

    Book the first appointment

    Tick it to book their first visit in the same step. It goes straight onto that person's day, no second step.

  14. 14

    When and with whom

    Date, From and To, and who goes; under it, what it's for and the type. With follows the Salesperson you picked.

  15. 15

    Save customer

    Saves the customer (and the appointment) and opens them. Press it yourself when it's real; the tour doesn't save.

  16. 16

    Close the form

    The X (or Cancel) closes the form without saving.

  17. 17

    Search customers

    Type part of a name, a phone (the last 4 digits work), street, email or C-code. It searches every customer, not only this page.

  18. 18

    Active, leads, customers…

    Active is leads and customers together. Leads, Customers, Inactive and All narrow the list; the numbers are the counts.

  19. 19

    Leads only

    Let's switch to Leads.

  20. 20

    New lead

    On Leads the button says New lead and the form starts as a lead. On Customers it starts as a customer.

  21. 21

    By salesperson

    Only the customers of one salesperson.

  22. 22

    By town

    Only one town. The number beside each town is how many customers are there.

  23. 23

    Quick filters

    Owes money (if you see money), Billed monthly, Open estimates, Do not market, or Demo records.

  24. 24

    The list

    Name, address and code, phone, stage, rep, open estimates, balance (money roles: red owed, green credit) and last activity. 50 a page, with Previous / Next below.

  25. 25

    Open a customer

    Click anyone to open their screen: details, history, estimates, jobs, invoices and more. "Everything on a customer" shows it all.
