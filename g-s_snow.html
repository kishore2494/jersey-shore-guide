Everything on Snow

23 steps Every button
This one is a step-by-step reference of every button on the screen: the pictures below show each one.

In the office app, press Help and type everything on snow: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Snow

    The snow log and snow billing: how many properties, visits logged and what is ready to bill.

  2. 2

    Log a visit

    For a visit the crew didn't log on the phone, or from the paper snow log.

  3. 3

    Property

    The property, from its snow plan.

  4. 4

    Date

    The day of the visit.

  5. 5

    Storm

    Name the storm to group its visits (e.g. February 8 storm). Blank groups them by date.

  6. 6

    Snowfall

    Inches at the property. Snowfall pricing uses the biggest per storm.

  7. 7

    Time in and out

    When the crew arrived and left, and how many men; hourly pricing uses them.

  8. 8

    Truck, driver, crew

    Which plow truck, who drove, and the crew.

  9. 9

    What was done

    Plowed the lot, Salted, Shoveled walks, Calcium.

  10. 10

    Salt, ice melt, loader

    How much salt and ice melt went down, and the loader hours.

  11. 11

    Site condition

    How it was left, e.g. Clear · icy spots by the door.

  12. 12

    Save the visit

    Log visit saves it to the log. The tour doesn't press it.

  13. 13

    Close the form

    The x closes the form without saving anything.

  14. 14

    Bill snow

    One draft invoice per customer for every unbilled visit on a priced plan (it asks first). Check and send them from Invoices.

  15. 15

    A storm

    Visits grouped by storm: the date, how many properties and visits, billed or not, and the biggest snowfall.

  16. 16

    A visit

    Property, times, hours, men, truck, driver, whether it came from the crew app, the site condition, what was done, and any photos.

  17. 17

    Change a visit

    Opens the visit in the same form.

  18. 18

    Remove a visit

    Removes it (it asks first). A billed visit shows its invoice number instead and can't change.

  19. 19

    Snow plans

    One plan per property, made on Maintenance (how often: Snow). New plan takes you there.

  20. 20

    Visits to bill

    Logged visits on that property not billed yet. A yellow banner warns when a plan has no pricing.

  21. 21

    Pricing

    How the property is billed: by snowfall or by the hour. Tour: "Price snow by snowfall or by the hour".

  22. 22

    To bill

    Each customer's total ready to bill, priced from their plan.

  23. 23

    See the lines

    Click a customer to see each visit's line and price.
