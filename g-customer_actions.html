Book, log a call or edit a customer

15 steps · 1:49 video

In the office app, press Help and type book, log a call or edit a customer: the tour plays on your own screen, with your own data.

  1. 1

    Open the customer

    It all starts from their screen. Let's open Bayview Marina.

  2. 2

    Book an appointment

    Appointment opens the booking form for this customer.

  3. 3

    Visit, phone call or task

    Pick what it is first and the fields follow: a call has a call time and who calls, a task can go to anyone. Then What for, Date, time and Notes.

  4. 4

    Who and where come along

    Their name, phone and address are filled in already, and With starts as their salesperson.

  5. 5

    Close it

    Close it without booking. Book it would put it on that person's day.

  6. 6

    Log a call

    Log call writes a conversation down on their Timeline.

  7. 7

    Call, note or email

    Phone call, Note or Email: pick what it was.

  8. 8

    What was said

    A line or two on what was said or agreed.

  9. 9

    Save it

    Save adds it to the Timeline with your name and the time. The tour doesn't press it.

  10. 10

    Close it

    The X closes it without saving.

  11. 11

    Edit their details

    Edit opens everything about them in one form.

  12. 12

    Change stage and billing

    The same fields as New customer. Move a lead to Customer or Inactive here, switch to monthly invoicing, change terms, salesperson or CSR.

  13. 13

    Save changes

    Saves and updates their screen. The tour doesn't save.

  14. 14

    Close it

    Closes the form; nothing changed.

  15. 15

    Start an estimate

    Estimate makes a new draft estimate for them and opens it to write. Press it when you're ready to quote.
